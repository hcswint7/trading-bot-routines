Imagine you've just found out your dad left you his traditional IRA. It can feel like a windfall, but at the end of the day there's a ticking clock. In order to stay onside with the rules, you need to know that the money is taxed by the IRS as ordinary income. Most non-spouse heirs must utilize the ten-year rule, which means the account has to be emptied within ten years of the death. So what does that mean for your wallet? It means you should really spread the withdrawals out to keep your tax bill lower.

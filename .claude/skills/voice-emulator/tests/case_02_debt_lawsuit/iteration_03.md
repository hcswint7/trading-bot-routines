If you ignore a lawsuit for unpaid credit card debt, the court will enter a default judgment against you, and that judgment lets the company collect through your paycheck or bank account. Once the company serves you with the lawsuit, you usually have twenty to thirty days to file an answer, and the deadline depends on your state. Miss that deadline and you lose automatically; the court never reviews the facts, and the company does not have to prove much. After a default judgment, the creditor can garnish your wages, freeze your bank account, or in some states place a lien on your property. Interest and court costs pile on top, so the amount you owe grows the longer you wait. You can sometimes ask the court to reopen a default judgment if you act quickly and show a good reason for missing the deadline, but courts do not always grant that, so answer the lawsuit before the deadline passes.

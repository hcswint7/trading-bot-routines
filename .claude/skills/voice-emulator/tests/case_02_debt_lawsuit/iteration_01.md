Imagine you get a letter saying a credit card company is suing you for an old debt. It can feel like a nightmare, but here's the thing — if you don't respond, you're in for a rude awakening. In order to avoid a default judgment, a response must be filed by the deadline, which is set by the court. What happens if you miss it? A default judgment is entered against you, and your wages can be garnished by the creditor. So really, at the end of the day, it's not just a paperwork problem, but a financial nightmare that will follow you around.

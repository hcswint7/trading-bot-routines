# Test question (finance / law)

> If I inherit my dad's traditional IRA, do I have to take the money out right
> away, and will I owe taxes on it?

This question is a good stress test for the voice emulator because it tempts a
model into story-like writing ("Imagine you've just inherited...") and into
jargon ("required beginning date," "eligible designated beneficiary"). The job:
answer it straight, in plain words, in five or more sentences, with the facts a
non-spouse beneficiary needs.

Target answer must:
- open by answering both parts of the question,
- be factually right (SECURE Act 10-year rule; withdrawals taxed as ordinary
  income; no early-withdrawal penalty on an inherited IRA),
- pass `audit.py` with zero HARD findings,
- read as a direct answer, not a story.

You cannot ignore a lawsuit for unpaid credit card debt without consequence: the court will enter a default judgment against you if you fail to respond. In order to avoid that outcome, you must file a response by the deadline the court sets, which usually falls twenty to thirty days after you are served. Once the court enters a default judgment, the creditor can garnish your wages, freeze your bank account, or in some states place a lien on your property. Interest and court costs pile on top, so the debt grows the longer you wait. You can sometimes ask the court to reopen a default judgment if you act quickly and have a good reason for missing the deadline, but courts do not always agree to that, so the safer move is to answer the lawsuit before the deadline passes.
